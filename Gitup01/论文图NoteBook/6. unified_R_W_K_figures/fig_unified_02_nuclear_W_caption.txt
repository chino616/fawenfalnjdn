Nuclear responses of Cs133 in the stored (p,n) basis. Panels (a-c) show M, Sigma-prime and Delta in the same channel colors as the R and K figures. Line styles distinguish pp, pn, nn, and np if independent. The Sigma-prime panel uses a symmetric-log scale (threshold 10^-6); the Delta panel uses a log scale. W has no neutrino-flavor or SM/NP indices. All nucleon pairs enter the full contractions.

Common numerical inputs: E_nu=30 MeV; C6u1[e,mu]=C6u1[mu,e]*=1e-05 GeV^-2; sin^2(theta_W)=0.23873; Tnr=0.05-13 keV; 160 identical nodes in all figures.
