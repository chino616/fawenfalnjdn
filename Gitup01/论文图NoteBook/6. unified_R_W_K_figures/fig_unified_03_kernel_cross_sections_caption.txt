Kernel and cross-section correspondence for the common Cs133, 30 MeV benchmark with C_1,e-mu^(6u)=C_1,mu-e^(6u)*=1e-05 GeV^-2. Panels (a,b) show the diagonal mu-mu and real off-diagonal e-mu channel kernels; panel (b) uses a signed symmetric-log scale with threshold 10^-18 GeV^-2. Panel (c) converts each SM+NP channel kernel to its pure incident nu_mu cross-section contribution. Panel (d) resolves SM nu_mu->nu_mu and FC NP nu_mu->nu_e, together with the total over all implemented responses and all final flavors. Final flavors are selected before response bilinears. The two exclusive contributions add without linear interference for this benchmark.

Common numerical inputs: E_nu=30 MeV; C6u1[e,mu]=C6u1[mu,e]*=1e-05 GeV^-2; sin^2(theta_W)=0.23873; Tnr=0.05-13 keV; 160 identical nodes in all figures.
