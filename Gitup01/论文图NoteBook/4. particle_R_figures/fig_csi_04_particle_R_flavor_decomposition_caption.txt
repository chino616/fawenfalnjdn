Particle-response functions in the proton-proton basis at E_nu = 30 MeV, using m_A = 133 m_amu and C_1,e-mu^(6u) = C_1,mu-e^(6u)* = 10^-5 GeV^-2. Panels (a,b) compare the real MM, Sigma-prime Sigma-prime and Delta Delta responses of the SM+NP model for the incoming-flavor mu-mu and e-mu entries. Panels (c,d) decompose R_MM into SM, NP and SM-NP interference contributions; panel (c) uses a logarithmic vertical axis. The diagonal interference vanishes to floating-point precision. In panel (d), the SM and NP entries vanish and the total equals the interference. The recoil range stays below the exact elastic endpoint. No nuclear responses or detector effects are included.
