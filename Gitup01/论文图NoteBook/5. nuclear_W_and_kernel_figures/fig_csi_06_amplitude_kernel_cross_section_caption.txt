Amplitude-squared flavor kernels and differential cross sections for Cs-133 at E_nu=30 MeV, with C_1,e-mu^(6u)=C_1,mu-e^(6u)*=10^-5 GeV^-2 and all other NP coefficients zero. Panels (a,b) show the mu-mu and real e-mu entries of the SM+NP M, Sigma-prime and Delta channel kernels after complete pp,pn,np,nn contraction. Panel (a) uses a log scale and panel (b) a signed symmetric-log scale (linear threshold 10^-18 GeV^-2). Panel (c) shows the SM, NP-only and SM+NP cross sections for a pure incident nu_mu, including every implemented channel. The diagonal SM-NP interference is zero to floating-point precision; the nonzero off-diagonal kernel does not contribute for this pure incident state.
