Nuclear responses of Cs-133 in the current proton-neutron table convention, shown as functions of nuclear recoil energy via y=(qb/2)^2 and q^2_abs=2*m_A*Tnr. Panels (a-c) show M, Sigma-prime and Delta responses, respectively, with pp, pn and nn entries. The np entries equal pn exactly for these tables. The Sigma-prime panel uses a signed symmetric-log scale (linear threshold 10^-6); the Delta panel uses a log scale. The responses carry no neutrino-flavor or SM/NP labels; no extra normalization is applied.
