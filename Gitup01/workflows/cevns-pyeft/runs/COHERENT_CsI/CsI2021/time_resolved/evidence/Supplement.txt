36      With this supplemental material, we provide selected, unbinned data events relevant for this result. Can-
37   didate waveforms are triggered from the accelerator, pulsed at 60 Hz with a FWHM of 378 ns. A sample
38   waveform is shown in Fig. 1 illustrating epochs in our waveform analysis. There are two regions of interest:
39   beam coincident (C) which encapsulates all beam-related activity and beam anti-coincident (AC) which
40   immediately precedes the arrival of the beam. Each region of interest has a 40 µs pretrace used to estimate
41   the background scintillation activity in the crystal on a spill-by-spill basis. Reconstructed events have a 3 µs
42   integration window. For each event, we reconstruct and observed energy in photoelectrons (PE) and recoil
43   time, trec .
44      We also provide signal parameters, neutron background distributions, and details of systematic uncertainty
45   to allow for future study of this analysis sample. All events selected with PE < 250 and 0 ≤ trec < 12 µs are
46   included. Events with energy 60 ≤ PE < 250 or trec ≥ 6 µs are not used for measuring the CEvNS cross
47   section but used for a search for light dark matter produced at the SNS.

55     Steady-state background : The beam-uncorrelated, steady-state background (SSBkg) can be estimated from
56   the AC data. For selected events, there is no correlation between Erec and trec , and thus the Erec distribution
57   can be determined using all selected events. The time distribution is exponential with a decay constant
58      • k = −(0.0494 ± 0.0061)/µs
59   determined by fitting the timing distribution of AC events.
60     Neutron backgrounds: We include both prompt beam-related neutron (BRN) and neutrino-induced neutron
61   (NIN) events. The reconstructed energy and time are uncorrelated, and so we include the one-dimensional
62   projections in PE and trec . For each, we provide the timing distribution in 0.01 µs bins normalized to the
63   beam exposure, 13.99 GWhr. We include the prediction for times −0.25 ≤ trec < 6.25 µs, to accommodate
64   uncertainty in beam timing. Due to this uncertainty in timing, the time dependent efficiency, εT , is not
